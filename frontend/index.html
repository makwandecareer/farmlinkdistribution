Pricing and payments

Clear commercial terms before any product moves.

FarmLink confirms the product price, distribution margin, logistics cost and payment terms on a formal quotation or invoice.

01

Product and distribution pricing

Buyers receive a consolidated price that may include the farmer's supply price, FarmLink's distribution margin and agreed logistics costs.

02

Delivery and logistics

Transport may be included in the quotation or shown separately based on route, volume, vehicle requirement and delivery frequency.

03

Buyer payment

Approved orders are paid by EFT or bank transfer using the banking details shown on the official FarmLink invoice. PayShap may be accepted where confirmed by the accounts team.

04

Farmer settlement

Approved suppliers are paid by bank transfer after the agreed delivery, acceptance and supporting documents have been verified.

Standard order workflow

  1. Submit an order or procurement requirement.
  2. Receive a written quotation and payment terms.
  3. Accept the quotation and pay the required deposit or invoice.
  4. FarmLink confirms supply, collection and delivery.
  5. Delivery is completed and the transaction is closed.

Account terms

  • New and once-off buyers normally pay before delivery.
  • Deposits may be required for large or scheduled orders.
  • Seven-day or 30-day accounts are considered only after business and credit approval.
  • No payment should be made to personal accounts or banking details not shown on an official FarmLink document.
