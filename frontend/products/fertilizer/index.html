Commercial requirements

What buyers should specify

A complete requirement improves supplier matching and quotation accuracy. For fertilizer, include crop, formulation, bag size, quantity, application requirements and destination.

Product specification

State the required grade, type, packaging format and any technical or quality requirements.

Volume and frequency

Provide the once-off or recurring quantity and the preferred collection or delivery schedule.

Delivery destination

Include the town, province, receiving requirements and required delivery date.

Product availability, pricing and delivery are not guaranteed by this page. FarmLink confirms supply only through a written quotation or invoice after reviewing the requirement.
